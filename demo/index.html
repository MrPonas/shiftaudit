How ShiftAudit calculates

Every entry becomes hours × base rate × multiplier, plus a night premium for the hours that fall inside your night window. Standby is paid at its own rate. Then the month is summed per category and compared with what you type in from your payslip.

Overtime work

Hours between start and end (rounded per your rule). The multiplier depends on the day: weekday overtime, rest day, or public holiday. Night hours inside the entry add the night premium on top.

Example: 3 h on a Saturday at €12 base, rest-day 2× → 3 × 12 × 2 = €72. If 1 h of it was after 22:00 with a 0.5 night premium → + 1 × 12 × 0.5 = €6. Total €78.

Standby / on-call (passive)

Time you must be reachable but are not working. Paid per hour as a percentage of base, a flat hourly amount, or a flat daily amount — whatever your contract says. No multipliers or night premium apply.

Call-out

Actual work done while on standby. Paid at the call-out multiplier, or the rest-day / holiday multiplier if that is higher. Shorter call-outs are lifted to your minimum call-out hours. Night premium applies. Tip: log the standby block and the call-outs separately — most contracts pay both.

Entries crossing midnight

If the end time is earlier than or equal to the start time, the entry ends the next day. The day type (weekday / rest day / holiday) is taken from the start date. For a long weekend standby, use the quick-add button — it creates one entry per day so each day is classified correctly.

Where is my data?

Only in this browser (local storage). Nothing is sent anywhere — there is no server. Use Backup (JSON) regularly and before switching devices or clearing browser data.

Rounding

Durations are rounded to the nearest step you choose (e.g. 15 min). Money is rounded to cents per entry, then summed.

Getting your missing pay

The report is designed to be handed to HR or payroll. Keep it factual: dates, hours, the rule you applied, the amount. Most gaps are honest mistakes — a missed timesheet line, a wrong multiplier, a holiday treated as a weekday.

  1. Fill in Pay rules from your contract or collective agreement.
  2. Log every overtime / standby / call-out block as it happens (takes 10 seconds).
  3. When the payslip arrives, type the extras into Summary.
  4. If the gap is real, Print / save PDF and use Copy message to HR.
