Questions

Is this legal or payroll advice?

No. ShiftAudit applies the rates you enter. Check your contract, collective agreement and local labour law, and use the report as a starting point for a conversation with HR.

Does it work for my country?

Yes, if your extras are computed as multiples of a base rate (the case almost everywhere). Multipliers, night window, weekend days, holidays and standby rules are all configurable.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser's local storage, on your device. There is no server and no account. Use the backup button to move between devices.

How do I "install" it?

Download the file and open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). Bookmark it. On a phone, open it from Files and "Add to Home Screen".

Can my team or company use it?

The personal license covers one person. For teams, unions or HR departments, write to us — a team license and a shared-rules setup are on the roadmap.

What if I find a bug or need a rule you don't support?

Email support and describe your contract rule. Reasonable requests ship as free v1.x updates.
